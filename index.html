Tuner reference pitch, A4 Hz

Turn on the microphone and play a note. The gauge shows how far off it is, and the trace under it shows the last few seconds. In an instrument mode, tap a string’s note to lock the tuner to it and hear it; ▲ ▼ beside it retune it by a semitone. Auto-detect follows whichever string you play. The dial beside the tuning picks the number of strings.

Pick an exercise and press Play, or the space bar. Set the tempo on the metronome: slide its weight or use − and +. Its knob sets the click volume (0 silences it) and its COUNT switch counts in a bar first. Scales and modes come in several positions along the neck. The amp shapes the sound.

Add a Guitar Pro (3–8) or MusicXML file; it stays in this browser. Drag across bars to loop them and slow down with the speed slider. Edit mode lets you change the tab and save a copy.
