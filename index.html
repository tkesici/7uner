Guitar level test

Tune your guitar on the Tuner tab first. Open a tab, play along with the metronome after the four-beat count-in, then rate yourself honestly. The highest tempo you can play cleanly and comfortably sets your level.

    Tip: the space bar starts and stops playback. To push yourself, turn off “Play the notes too” and play against the metronome alone.

    Interval recognition

    Two notes play one after the other. Pick the distance between them. 10 questions; replay as often as you like.

    Play what you hear

    First listen to the reference A (6th string, 5th fret). Then listen to the short phrase and find it on your guitar. When you think you have it, reveal the tab to check. Everything is in A minor pentatonic.

    One-minute improvisation

    Start the A drone and play A minor pentatonic for a full minute without stopping. Recording yourself on your phone helps a lot: on playback you’ll hear whether you fall into repetition and whether your phrases breathe.
